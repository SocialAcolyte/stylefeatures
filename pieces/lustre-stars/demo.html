Shahnameh

The Persian Book of Kings, Abolqasem Ferdowsi, translated by Dick Davis

4.2 of 5from 1,240 readers

Ferdowsi finished his poem around 1010, after thirty years: some fifty thousand couplets of the kings of Iran, from the first men to the Arab conquest, with Rostam and his horse Rakhsh at the heart of it. Two and a half centuries later the potters of Kashan were still writing its verses round the borders of the tiles they made for shrines and palaces.
