## Images and media

- **The Penitent Magdalen**, Georges de La Tour (French, Vic-sur-Seille 1593-1652 Lunéville), ca. 1640. <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436839>  
  Licence: CC0 (Met Open Access, public domain).  
  Changes: Cropped to the painted canvas and scaled to 1800 px wide. magdalen-unlit.webp is a patch with both painted flames removed (masked and filled from the neighbouring mirror glass); magdalen-light.webp is a derived light-influence map; magdalen-grain.webp is a high-pass of the canvas texture round the mirror; magdalen.json is the rig (flame positions, mirror clip). Prepared offline with Python/Pillow.  
  File: `assets/magdalen.webp, assets/magdalen-unlit.webp, assets/magdalen-light.webp, assets/magdalen-grain.webp, assets/magdalen.json`
