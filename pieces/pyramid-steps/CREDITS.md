## Images and media

- **Pyramidion of Iufaa**, Late Period, Dynasty 26, 664-525 BCE. Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Rogers Fund, 1921 (21.2.66), Open Access. <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/552805>  
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain).  
  Changes: Four faces, each from the Met view that looks straight at it (the four colour views), matted with BiRefNet, then each mapped by an affine fit of its own measured base corners and apex onto the true face triangle (slant / base 1.416 from the catalogue dimensions; the photos measure 1.36-1.38, i.e. the camera about 14 degrees above the face normal). The four black-and-white corner views were used to check which faces adjoin. The museum's painted accession number on face A was inpainted out. Mid-tones opened slightly (gamma 0.8): the Met shot it on black. Tile order in the atlas: A scarab in the solar barque, C Iufaa kneeling under the jackal (three columns), B sun disc between adoring baboons, D Iufaa kneeling under the jackal (two columns).  
  File: `assets/capstone.webp`
- **Specimen of Mortar from the Great Pyramid**. Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art (17.6.143), Open Access, via Wikimedia Commons. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Speciman_of_Mortar_from_the_Great_Pyramid_MET_17.6.143_EGDP017920.jpg>  
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain).  
  Changes: A square from the middle of the sample (gypsum mortar, pink with lime and grit), spread thinly on the bed of the course being laid.  
  File: `assets/mortar.webp`
- **Course heights of the Great Pyramid, courses 1-203, north-east corner**. Source: W. M. Flinders Petrie, The Pyramids and Temples of Gizeh (London, 1883), pl. VIII, as tabulated at cheops-pyramide.ch (Stone courses of the Pyramid of Khufu). <https://cheops-pyramide.ch/khufu-pyramid/stonecourses-pyramid.html>  
  Changes: Summed, the 203 courses give 138.6 m, Petrie's measured height.  
  File: `assets/(data in pyramid-steps.js)`
