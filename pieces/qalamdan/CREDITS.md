Every part is a real photograph of a real object, cut out rigidly: a matte, a uniform scale, a crop, a turn. Nothing is redrawn, recoloured or warped. Sizes come from each museum's own measurements. The tray is laid out at 74 px to the centimetre, and each tool is placed in it at its catalogued length.
