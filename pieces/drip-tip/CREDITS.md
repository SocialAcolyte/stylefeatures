## Images and media

- **The Equatorial Jungle**, Henri Rousseau, 1909. National Gallery of Art, Washington. <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Henri_Rousseau,_The_Equatorial_Jungle,_1909,_NGA_46688.jpg>  
  Licence: CC0 (NGA Open Access).  
  Changes: 3681 x 4000; greyed in the rain at runtime; shown cropped to its upper half  
  File: `assets/rousseau-the-equatorial-jungle.webp`
- **The Equatorial Jungle: outlines of fourteen of its leaves**, Henri Rousseau (outlines traced for this component), 1909. Source: derived from the file above.  
  Licence: CC0.  
  Changes: alpha of each cut-out leaf, with a 5 px collar of the surrounding paint and a fine wedge past the tip  
  File: `assets/leaf-masks.png`
- **The Equatorial Jungle: the paint behind those leaves, filled in**, Henri Rousseau (inpainted for this component), 1909. Source: derived from the file above.  
  Licence: CC0.  
  Changes: Telea inpainting of each leaf's area plus 4 px, from the paint around it; only seen in slivers while a leaf swings  
  File: `assets/leaf-under.webp`
