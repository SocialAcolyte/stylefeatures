## From the README

No libraries (`tilt.js` is the house helper, copied in). The dishes are from
The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection (CC0): *Dish with
Hydrangeas* (Japan, Nabeshima type, ca. 1690–1730), *Large Dish with Plants*
(Japan, Arita ware, 1640s) and *Bowl with two fish* (China, Longquan ware,
13th century), cut out from their grey grounds. Also from the Met (CC0): a
fluted coffee cup (ca. 1770–80), a pheasant plate (ca. 1763), a red raku tea
bowl (18th c., Japan), a turquoise vase with scroll handles (ca. 1760–65) and a
lidded Imari baluster vase (ca. 1690–1720). These are photographed from the
side or at an angle, not from above, and still break and mend well (a handle or
a neck is just part of the outline). Five Indian bronzes, also Met CC0: a Shiva
Nataraja (Tamil Nadu, late 11th c.), a seated Ganesha (12th–13th c.), Hanuman
(11th c.), a Durga shrine (Himachal Pradesh, 12th c.) and a standing Vishnu
(ca. 970s). They are sculptures, so they use the flat form; the gold stays on
the metal and the Nataraja's ring is left open. See `assets/credits.json`.
