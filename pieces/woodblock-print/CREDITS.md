## Images and media

- **South Wind, Clear Sky (Gaifū kaisei), also known as Red Fuji, from the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku sanjūrokkei)**, Katsushika Hokusai, ca. 1830–32. <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/55736>  
  Licence: CC0 (Met Open Access, public domain).  
  Changes: Cropped inside the trimmed sheet edge and resized to 2000 px; the blocks file is our own separation of this image into six coverage maps (key, pale ground, red, sky, forest, summit).  
  File: `assets/red-fuji.webp, assets/red-fuji-blocks.webp, assets/red-fuji.json`
- **Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge and Atake (Ōhashi Atake no yūdachi), from the series One Hundred Famous Views of Edo (Meisho Edo hyakkei)**, Utagawa Hiroshige, 1857. <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/55433>  
  Licence: CC0 (Met Open Access, public domain).  
  Changes: Cropped to the sheet with its seals and resized to 1600 px wide; the blocks file is our own separation into twelve coverage maps (key, yellow, red, green, river, sky, far shore, piers, deep water, dusk bokashi, two rain blocks).  
  File: `assets/sudden-shower.webp, assets/sudden-shower-blocks.webp, assets/sudden-shower.json`
