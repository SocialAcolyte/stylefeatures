## Images and media

- **The Red Carp**, Utagawa Toyokuni I, c. 1804/18. Source: Art Institute of Chicago, Clarence Buckingham Collection, 1925.3308 (object 24886). <https://www.artic.edu/artworks/24886/the-red-carp>  
  Licence: CC0 (Art Institute of Chicago, public domain).  
  Changes: A top view of a koi assembled from the print: scales from the carp's flank; head skin from its pale snout and red brow (smooth, unscaled); the tail from two copies of its pectoral fin. Eyes, nostrils and barbels drawn over it in the print's own colours. Recoloured to kohaku, sanke, yamabuki and chagoi in the shader.  
  File: `assets/koi-body.webp`
