Every part of the board is a real photograph of a real object, cut out rigidly: a crop, a matte, a uniform scale. Nothing is redrawn, recoloured or warped. The board and its pieces are one object (Met 01.4.1a-p) from one documentation campaign (EGDP0136xx), all straight down. The piece is scaled against the board's tiles by the ratio measured on the Met's three-quarter view DP310299, where the same pieces stand on the same board.
