## Images and media

- **Mimosa pudica (Sensitive Plant), plate 544**, Henry C. Andrews (drawn, engraved and hand-coloured), 1807. <https://www.plantillustrations.org/illustration.php?id_illustration=111905>  
  Licence: Public domain (published 1807).  
  Changes: Trimmed 14 px (gutter) and 30 px (gilt edge) off the scan's left and top; resized from 2872 to 2200 px wide (Lanczos).  
  File: `assets/plate.webp`
- `assets/mimosa-leaf.webp`: derived from `plate.webp`. the plate's great lower leaf, its colour un-mixed from the paper under it (RGB) and its coverage (alpha), in the leaf's box Licence: Public domain.
- `assets/mimosa-clean.webp`: derived from `plate.webp`. the same box with the leaf lifted off: paper tone and grain copied from the plate's own bare paper, the stalk's overlap on other leaves reconstructed (OpenCV FSR); alpha marks the lifted area Licence: Public domain.
- `assets/mimosa-ids.png`: derived from `plate.webp`. piece id per pixel: petiole and hub, 4 rachises, 255 leaflets Licence: Public domain.
- `assets/mimosa.json`: derived from `plate.webp`. the pieces: pulvinules (pivots), axes, sides, ranks along each pinna; pinna paths; primary pulvinus and hub Licence: Public domain.
