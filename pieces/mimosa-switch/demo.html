Sensitive plant

Touch-me-not

One leaf of Mimosa pudica is the switch. Open, it holds its pinnae out like fingers. Switch it off and the signal runs down the leaf: the leaflets fold pair by pair, the pinnae close, and the leaf drops.

Folding takes a moment. Reopening takes longer. Toggle it fast and the plant stops bothering, though the switch still changes.
