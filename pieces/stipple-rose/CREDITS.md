## Images and media

- **Les Roses: Rosa Lucida (Rosier luisant)**, after Pierre-Joseph Redouté (P. J. Redouté pinx.); engraved by Bessin; printed by Rémond; text by C.-A. Thory, 1817-1824. The Cleveland Museum of Art, Mr. and Mrs. Charles G. Prasse Collection, 1965.290 (the museum's record names Henri Joseph Redouté; the plate is signed P. J. Redouté pinx.). <https://clevelandart.org/art/1965.290>  
  Licence: CC0 (Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access).  
  Changes: rose.webp is the scan resampled to 2000 px wide. The other files are our separation of it: the watercolour layer, the paper, the engraved dots (found one by one in the 400 dpi scan), the poupée and wash maps.  
  File: `assets/rose.webp`

## From the README

*Les Roses: Rosa Lucida*, P. J. Redouté pinx., Bessin sculp., Imprimerie de Rémond,
1817-24, stipple and line engraving printed in colour and finished by hand. The
Cleveland Museum of Art, Mr. and Mrs. Charles G. Prasse Collection, 1965.290, Open Access
(CC0). The separation into print and watercolour, the dots, and the maps are ours. No
third-party code.
