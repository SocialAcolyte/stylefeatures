## From the README

Zhou Chen 周臣 (c. 1450–c. 1536), *Beggars and Street Characters*, 1516, handscroll, ink and light colour on paper,
31.9 x 244.5 cm. The Cleveland Museum of Art, John L. Severance Fund, 1964.94, CC0. Frontispiece and title slip by Zhao
Heqin (1962); colophons by Huang Jishui (1564), Zhang Fengyi and Wen Jia (1577). Translations of the inscriptions:
Cleveland's own record. The demo's press, Idle Window, is made up; its name is from Zhou's note ("閒窗", idle at my
window). See `assets/credits.json`.
