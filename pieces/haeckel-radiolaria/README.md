# haeckel-radiolaria

Haeckel's radiolaria plate inks in radially as you scroll, from pale line to the full Kunstformen plate of 1899.

## The real thing

Ernst Haeckel drew the skeletons of radiolaria — single-celled creatures that build themselves glass houses of perfect geometry — for Kunstformen der Natur (1899), and drew them as an architect would: every spine and lattice placed to show the underlying order. Letting the plate arrive radially, centre outwards, honours how these organisms themselves grow, secreting their silica skeletons outward from a central capsule. The last frame is the plate untouched.

## How it works

Layered copies of one scan are stacked in a pinned frame on a scroll runway, the full plate revealed with a soft radial depth as progress grows, driven by a damped spring that follows and reverses the scroll. Reduced motion shows the finished plate. `haeckelRadiolaria(runway, opts)` returns a handle with `destroy()`.
