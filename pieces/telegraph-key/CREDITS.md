## From the README

*Morse–Vail Telegraph Key*, 1844, Alfred Vail for Samuel F. B. Morse. Smithsonian National Museum of American History,
EM.181411, from Western Union Telegraph Co. Photograph NMAH-JN2014-3911, CC0 (Smithsonian Open Access). The 3D scan of
the same key (Smithsonian 3D, CC0) was used to measure. Other NMAH photographs of the key (88-8830-12, SIA-74-2491,
MAH-27979) were used to read the trunnion. Sounds: SoundsofTheMachine, Freesound 797930, CC0. See `assets/credits.json`
and `assets/PICK.md` (the hunt: 147 images of about 40 keys).

Reference: Frank L. Pope, *Modern Practice of the Electric Telegraph* (New York, 1869 and later), §52, "The Morse
Signal Key".
